# ProjectTabi Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

ProjectTabi is a local-first pilgrimage planning and visit-record app. This policy describes the data it uses and where that data goes.

## Local data

Plans, points, visit records, photos, cached reference images, settings, sync timestamps, and sync history are stored on your device. Sync history is local to each device and is not sent as a separate report. WebDAV credentials can be stored in the device credential store.

## WebDAV sync

When you configure WebDAV and choose a sync direction, ProjectTabi transmits the selected plans, records, and referenced image bytes to or from the WebDAV server you configured. The server operator can access data stored on that server under their own service terms and privacy policy. ProjectTabi does not operate that server. You control its address and credentials.

Sync failure details remain on screen until dismissed or replaced; they are not automatically sent to the app authors. You can copy them yourself when requesting support.

## Device permissions

ProjectTabi may request:

- Camera access to take pilgrimage photos.
- Photos or file access to import images and plan packages, and save comparison images.
- Location access to show your current map position and record locations.
- Microphone permission descriptions required by some camera libraries; ProjectTabi does not use microphone audio for analytics.
- Network access for WebDAV, map tiles, Bangumi, Anitabi, and services you configure.

## Third-party services

When you use related features, your device may connect directly to the configured map provider, Anitabi, Bangumi, a WebDAV server, or an external navigation app. Those services may receive ordinary network information such as your IP address and requested resources. Their own terms and privacy policies apply.

## Import and export

MiriaGo JSON plan files, .sjhplan packages, CSV files, photos, and comparison images are read or written only when you choose to import, export, save, or share them. ProjectTabi does not automatically publish exported files.

## Analytics and advertising

ProjectTabi does not include advertising SDKs and does not intentionally send analytics to an app-operated backend.

## Data deletion and contact

Delete local records in the app where supported, or clear ProjectTabi's app data through your device or operating system. WebDAV copies and exported files must be deleted separately from those services or locations.

For privacy questions or bug reports, use ProjectTabi Issues: https://github.com/Ch1Zume/ProjectTabi/issues

# ProjectTabi 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 9 月 25 日

ProjectTabi 是一款优先在本地保存数据的圣地巡礼计划与记录应用。本政策说明应用使用的数据以及数据会传到哪里。

## 本地数据

计划、点位、巡礼记录、照片、参考图缓存、设置、同步时间戳和同步历史保存在设备本地。同步历史只保存在本机，不会作为独立报告上传。WebDAV 凭据可以保存在设备的凭据存储中。

## WebDAV 同步

配置 WebDAV 并选择同步方向后，ProjectTabi 会将所选计划、记录及关联图片传到你配置的 WebDAV 服务器，或从该服务器下载。服务器运营者可依其服务条款和隐私政策访问服务器上存储的数据。ProjectTabi 不运营该服务器，服务器地址和账号由你自行配置。

同步错误详情只会显示在应用中，不会自动发送给开发者。你可以在反馈问题时主动复制并提交。

## 系统权限

ProjectTabi 可能会请求：

- 相机：拍摄巡礼照片。
- 照片或文件访问：导入图片和计划包，保存对比图。
- 定位：显示地图上的当前位置并记录地点。
- 麦克风权限说明：部分相机库可能要求系统显示该权限说明；ProjectTabi 不会将麦克风音频用于分析。
- 网络：连接 WebDAV、地图瓦片、Bangumi、Anitabi 及你自行配置的服务。

## 第三方服务

使用相关功能时，设备可能直接连接地图服务商、Anitabi、Bangumi、WebDAV 服务器或外部导航应用。这些服务可能收到 IP 地址、请求资源等常规网络信息。请参阅相应服务的条款和隐私政策。

## 导入与导出

MiriaGo JSON 计划文件、.sjhplan 数据包、CSV、照片和对比图，只会在你选择导入、导出、保存或分享时读取或写入。ProjectTabi 不会自动发布导出文件。

## 分析与广告

ProjectTabi 不包含广告 SDK，也不会主动向应用自有后端发送分析数据。

## 删除数据与联系方式

你可以在应用支持的范围内删除本地记录，也可以通过设备或操作系统清除 ProjectTabi 的应用数据。WebDAV 副本和应用外保存的导出文件需要在对应服务器或位置另行删除。

如有隐私问题或需要反馈 Bug，请使用 ProjectTabi Issues：https://github.com/Ch1Zume/ProjectTabi/issues
